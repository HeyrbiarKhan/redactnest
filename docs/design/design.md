**Rewritten**: 2026-11-26, for spec [0026](../specs/0026-ui-redesign/index.md) (its AC-18): white and grey surfaces in place of the warm off white, the teal accent at `#0f766e`, shadcn/ui's component pattern under our own tokens, small CSS motion, and the 14px floor kept. ## Build mandate

You are a senior product designer shipping a real product surface, at the bar in the `/develop` UI guide: brand, real product copy, a considered layout, every state (empty, loading, error), and a footer. This product's version of complete is calm and uncluttered rather than busy. Every element earns its place, and nothing is added that the governing spec did not ask for. Compose from the primitives in `src/ui/`; a page that needs a piece they do not have adds it there, with its tests, rather than inline. A mockup gives way wherever it would loosen a lint rule, a contrast threshold or the text floor, and every line that describes the engine holds under spec 0018 AC-5 and spec 0024's claim register, whatever the mockup's draft said.

## Character & direction

- **White and grey, never warm.** The page and the cards are white (`canvas`, `surface`); `subtle` takes quiet bands and `/tool`'s page; `muted` takes chips, count badges and hover fills. Depth comes from a 1px `border` hairline and the lightest card shadow. The larger shadows appear in five places only (the drawn product window, its floating card, the Pro summary card, the account menu and the page in `/tool`'s preview), and only the window and the Pro card are tinted teal (`tests/unit/brand-source.test.ts` holds the first four). A shadow is depth, never a colour role, so it is the one place an alpha colour is allowed, and it never sits under text.
- **One accent, used sparingly.** Teal (`accent`) marks the primary action, a ticked box and its row's `accent-faint` fill, links, line icons and the mark in its tile. It fills a whole section twice only: the closing cards on `/` and on the guides, where the focus ring turns `ink`. Red is not a second accent: it marks the two buttons that delete an account, and on `/tool` a refusal's icon tile on a white card, never a button (spec 0013, INV-12, as spec 0026 AC-12 amends it).
- **One dark band.** The network band on `/` is `night`, with its own ink, muted, accent, raised and edge tokens, and its focus ring is `night-accent`.
- **Honest type.** Inter only, and every word at 14px or more: the mockups' 9 to 13 pixel lines are `text-small`, and their layouts give them the room. The scale adds `hero` (the home `h1` only), `display`, `headline`, `intro`, `lede`, `subhead`, `copy` and `price` for the larger pages. Only `hero`, `display` and `headline` step down below `sm`, through breakpoints, never viewport units; the other five keep one size at every width. The monospace stack appears only in named places (`data-mono`): the two black box copies and the network table on `/`.
- **Small motion.** Three durations (`fast` 120ms, `base` 200ms, `slow` 320ms) and one ease out curve, in these places only: the account menu opening (a fade with a 4 pixel rise), a checklist group opening and closing, the progress bar's fill, a progress step's tick, a home question's chevron, and colour and edge changes on hover and press. One rule under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` makes every transition and animation instant, and no component opts out. Nothing moves the layout under a focused control.
- **Tone carries more than colour.** Every warning, note and error has its own icon shape and a hidden tone word; a highlighted match is also semibold; links are always underlined, except the header's nav items, which sit in a labelled row and mark the current page with `aria-current`, semibold text and an accent bar (spec 0013, AC-7).

## Component & usage rules (do's and don'ts)

- Do use a token for every colour. Don't use Tailwind's default palette (it is wiped), an arbitrary colour (`bg-[#…]`), an alpha modifier (`text-ink/70`) or an `oklch()` value; lint rejects the first three. A quieter text colour is `ink-muted`, and a caption on a light fill is `ink-subtle`, never on `muted`, where it measures under 4.5:1. `#a1a1aa` is never text on a light surface.
- Do add a new foreground and background pairing to spec 0003's contract and to `tests/unit/contrast.test.ts` in the same change that introduces it. A pairing that fails is fixed by changing the pairing, never by lowering a threshold.
- Do build a primitive in shadcn/ui's pattern under our rules: variants through `class-variance-authority` (`cva`), every class a token utility, and a Radix primitive only where a widget needs managed focus and keyboard behaviour (today one, the account menu behind `DropdownMenu`). Don't copy a shadcn file with its theme: no `text-xs`, no alpha modifier, no `cursor-` class, no oklch variable. `src/ui` stays presentation only, and the caller passes everything a primitive shows.
- Do use `Button` for anything that acts or navigates like a button: `primary` for the one main action in view, `secondary` beside it, `ghost` for a quiet boxed action, `link` for an action drawn as an underlined link, `inverse` on the accent cards. Sizes `default` (40px), `md` (44px), `lg` (48px), `xl` (50px) and `icon` (a 40px square whose words are visually hidden); heights are minimums, never fixed. Red is for deleting an account and nothing else: `danger`, solid red with white words, for the step that destroys it, and `danger-secondary`, a red outline, for the button that opens that step.
- Do make a button `busy` while its work keeps the visitor waiting: it shows a spinner before its working words, keeps focus and its rest colours, and drops every press until the work ends, and a visually hidden polite region that was in the page from the first render says the same words. Don't disable it instead, because a disabled button drops focus to the page (spec 0013, AC-34 and INV-13).
- Don't use `next/link` to enter or leave `/tool`, or for any link to Pricing, an account page, Subscribe or Manage billing. Leaving `/tool` has to be a real page load so `pagehide` ends the session (spec 0002, INV-6), entering should be one so the tool runs under its own strict content security policy, and nothing should prefetch a page that starts a checkout.
- Radius: `rounded-item` (8px) on menu items, checkboxes and small links; `rounded-control` (10px) on buttons, icon tiles and the plan strip; `rounded-card` (14px) on cards and callouts; `rounded-panel` (16px) on the home and guide cards, the drop zone and `/tool`'s state cards; `rounded-window` (18px) on the drawn window; `rounded-band` (24px) on the closing cards; `rounded-full` on badges, chips, avatars and pills. A tile is never rounder than the card it sits in.
- Icons come from `lucide-react`, imported by name: line icons with a stroke of 1.75 or heavier, most at 20px, and hidden from assistive technology unless they are the only label.
- Don't set a cursor in a component. One rule in `src/app/globals.css` gives whatever can be clicked the pointer (menu items included) and whatever is disabled `not-allowed`, Clerk's card included, and lint rejects a `cursor-` utility (spec 0013, AC-33; spec 0026, AC-15).
- Document derived text reaches a primitive only as a prop and renders only as React text. Never `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`, never `document.title`, never the URL. On `/tool` the file's name is header text only.
- The `frontend-design` skill guides any axis these rules and the specs leave free. Where spec 0026 or spec 0003 pins a choice (Inter alone, the white surfaces, the one teal, the radii, the 14px floor), the spec wins.

## Responsive & accessibility direction

- WCAG 2.2 AA on every page (spec 0026, AC-16), proved in a real browser in `tests/e2e/design-system.spec.ts`, `account-slot.spec.ts` and `legal-pages.spec.ts`: axe at desktop width, at 320 pixels and in forced colours; no sideways scroll at 320 pixels, and none at 320 with 200% text on `/`, the guides, Pricing and the pages of prose; nothing clipped at 200% text; a visible ring on every control Tab reaches, in page order; every target at least 24 by 24; and the account menu and the progress steps walked by keyboard with the names a screen reader reads. `/sign-out`, which needs a real session, is walked on `pnpm dev`.
- Focus is a 2px `focus` outline with a 2px offset on every control, from the base `:focus-visible` rule; on the night band it is `night-accent` and on the accent cards `ink`, because `focus` falls under 3:1 on both. A menu item draws it flush to its edge, inside the menu. Only `main`, as the skip link's target, drops it.
- Every target is at least 24 by 24, and every button 40 tall; in a checklist row the whole row is the checkbox's label and target. A link inside a sentence is sized by its line, as WCAG 2.5.8 allows; a link alone on its line is held to 24 pixels like a footer link.
- What sticks, each only from `lg`: `/`'s header, whose top scroll padding keeps a focused control clear of it; on `/tool`, the action panel (in a window at least 36rem tall), kept clear of focus by scroll margins and a `focusin` fallback, and the page preview's frame while it shows; and the On this page list beside the text of the guides, the legal pages and Security. The preview's frame and the lists each sit in their own column, so they cover nothing.
- In forced colours mode, filled controls keep a transparent border so the system paints an edge, the checkbox returns to the native control, the highlighted match uses the system `Highlight` pair, the action panel and the account menu keep their edges where their shadows go, and the preview's boxes keep their edge and lose their fill.
- No text size in viewport units, no fixed height on anything that holds text, and a word too long for a line alone breaks rather than pushing the page sideways.
