What redacting a PDF should do

Redacting a PDF should take the words out, not only hide them. A box drawn over text can leave the words in the file, where they can still be copied or found.

In RedactNest, the selectable text you tick is taken out of the page, then marked where it was.

Scans are a picture. Words that are only part of a picture are not read, so they can stay in the new file.

Look over the whole document before you share the new file, not only what RedactNest listed and you ticked. What it can miss
